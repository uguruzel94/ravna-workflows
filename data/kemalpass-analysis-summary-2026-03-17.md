## Email Draft Quality

**Turkish copy rules applied:**
- Formal "Siz" throughout
- Concrete specifics: facility size, provinces served, process names
- No clichés: eşsiz, güçlü, yenilikçi forbidden
- Action verbs: azaltır, kurtarır, otomatikleştirir, hızlandırır
- Subject: 8-12 words, concrete, no question mark
- Body: 150-200 words, one specific detail + one opportunity + CTA
- Close: 45-min free consultation offer, day options (Çarşamba/Perşembe)

**Example (Kanaat Demir):**
```
Subject: Kanaat Demir'de üretim verimliliğini 30% artırın
[Specific detail: 10,000 m² facility, founder name]
[Opportunity: order-to-invoice automation saves 10-12 hrs/week]
[CTA: free 45-min consultation, date picker]
```
